12 Geometry and Transformations
===============================

Recipes
-------

* 2D Translation: Moves points by x and y offsets.
* 2D Rotation: Rotates points using the current angle mode.
* 2D Scaling and Shearing: Composes two affine transforms.
* 2D Reflection: Reflects points across coordinate axes.
* 2D Composition and Inversion: Builds and reverses a reusable transform.
* 3D Translation and Scaling: Moves and resizes 3D points.
* 3D Axis Rotations: Rotates around x, y, and z.
* 3D Plane Reflections: Reflects across xy, xz, and yz planes.
* 3D Composition and Inversion: Composes and reverses 3D transforms.
* Points, Distances, and Matrices: Connects geometry to vectors and matrices.
